## Personal Todo
```dataview
TASK
FROM "1-projects/Todo" OR "1-projects/Finance" OR "1-projects/Math" OR "1-projects/Software"
WHERE
    !completed
    AND !contains(lower(text), "cancelled")
    AND !regexmatch(
        "1-projects/Finance/(screener|holdings)|Investment Checklist|Seesel - BMP checklist",
        file.path
    )
    AND (start and start <= date(this.file.name))
```
